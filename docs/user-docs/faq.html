1 · Getting started

The basics — read this first
?

What is IBG Invoice, in one sentence?

It's the app that turns "we sold them a unit and now they need to pay" into a clean PDF invoice with a real number — and remembers it forever.

Before this app, people made invoices in Word, saved them in random folders, and tried to remember the numbers. This app does all that for you.

?

Who can use it?

Anyone with a login. There are two roles:

  • User — that's most people. You can make invoices, mark them paid, add clients, look things up. (Like a teacher.)
  • Super admin — same as User plus the back-office stuff: add new companies, set up bank accounts, create new logins, edit help articles. (Like the school principal.)

If you don't see a button this guide mentions, you're probably a User and that button is for Super admins. Ask one.

?

How do I log in?

Open the app's URL → enter your email and password. Done. There's no "Sign up" — Super admin makes accounts, then sends you the login.

?

I don't have a password yet — what do I do?

Ask a Super admin to create your account. They'll either send you the password or set it up so you can pick one on first login.

2 · Making invoices

Drafts, templates, and line items
?

How do I make a new invoice?

  1. Click Invoices on the left sidebar.
  2. Click + New invoice in the top-right corner.
  3. Pick a template (it auto-fills the company and bank for you).
  4. Pick the client from the dropdown — or click + Quick add if they're new.
  5. Set the issue date. The due date fills itself in (5 days later).
  6. Add at least one line item — usually one is enough.
  7. Click Save draft.
Heads up: A draft has no number yet. The number gets stamped on later, when you click Issue. That's so accounting numbers stay tidy.
?

What's a "template" exactly?

A template is a shortcut. It says: "Use this company, this bank account, and this currency." Saves you typing the same info every time.

We have nine templates because we have nine companies. The most common one is IBG THB.

  • IBG THB · IB Group THB — Thai company, paid in baht.
  • IB Group USD — offshore company, paid in dollars.
  • Wise THB · Crypto · IBG Kas — special payment rails. Use only when you know.
  • Blank — fully manual. Pick everything yourself.
?

What are "line items"?

Each line item is one row on the invoice — basically "what are you charging the client for". There are three flavours:

  • Commission — selling price × percent + corrections. Use this for most property deals.
  • Bonus — flat amount. Use this for one-off bonuses.
  • Other — free-form. Write what it's for in Project and the amount in Other amount.

Most invoices have one line. Add more only if the same invoice covers multiple items.

?

What about VAT and WHT?

Two checkboxes on the form:

  • VAT 7% — turn on for Thai-resident invoices that need VAT. Pick "VAT included" if the price already includes VAT, or leave it off if VAT is added on top.
  • WHT 3% — withholding tax. Turn on if the client withholds 3% from the payment.

Not sure? Ask accounting. Don't guess — VAT settings affect what the client owes.

?

Can I save and finish later?

Yes. Click Save draft any time. Drafts live in the main list with a "draft" badge — open one and keep editing. Drafts never expire.

3 · Issuing — making it real

Locking the number, freezing the rate
?

What does "Issue" actually do?

Five things, all in one click:

  1. Stamps a real invoice number on it (e.g. 23/04/2026-0001).
  2. Locks today's exchange rate (only if the invoice converts currencies).
  3. Generates a polished PDF.
  4. Backs the PDF up to Google Drive.
  5. Changes the status from "Draft" to "Issued".
?

Where's the "Issue" button?

Open any draft. There's a big black Issue button right under the totals.

Don't confuse "Generate PDF" with "Issue". Generate PDF on a draft just makes a preview file. Only Issue publishes the invoice with a real number you can send to a client.
?

Can I pick the number myself instead of letting it auto-pick?

Yes. On the draft, scroll down to Advanced → fill in the full invoice number field with whatever format you want (e.g. 23/04/2026-0001). When you click Issue, the app uses that number instead of auto-picking.

Useful when you're migrating an old invoice from another system. The auto-counter still ticks forward in the background, so future invoices stay sequential.

?

I clicked Issue and got a yellow note about "PDF generation failed". Now what?

The invoice is issued — that part worked. Only the PDF didn't render. Click the Regenerate PDF button on the invoice page to retry. Almost always works the second time.

4 · Marking as paid + receipts

When the money actually arrives
?

The client paid. What do I do?

Open the issued invoice → click the green Mark as paid (+ receipt) button. The app asks for the payment date (defaults to today). After confirming:

  • The invoice flips to Paid.
  • A new receipt is born — same number plus -R (e.g. 23/04/2026-0001-R).
  • Both PDFs are made and backed up.
  • The receipt shows up under Receipts in the sidebar.
?

Do I need to make the receipt myself?

No, never. The app makes it for you the second you click Mark as paid. If you find yourself "making a receipt" by hand, you're doing it wrong — talk to a Super admin.

?

The client paid yesterday, not today. Does the date matter?

Yes — pick the actual payment date when prompted. The receipt PDF will show that date. If you typed today by accident, just edit the invoice afterwards (see "Editing & fixing mistakes").

?

How do I send the receipt to the client?

Open the receipt → click Copy public link. Paste that link into your email or CRM. The link is permanent and unguessable — anyone with the link can open the PDF, no login needed.

5 · Editing & fixing mistakes

Yes, you can fix issued invoices
?

Can I edit an issued invoice?

Yes. Open the invoice → click Edit. A yellow banner reminds you it's already published. Make your change → Save. The PDF re-generates automatically. If the invoice was paid, the receipt PDF re-generates too.

The invoice number, exchange rate and full audit trail are preserved — they don't change.

?

Can I edit a paid invoice?

Yes — same as above. The receipt that came with it will re-render with the new amounts.

Use this carefully on paid invoices. If accounting already booked the original amount, you might need to tell them about the change. Editing fixes the file but doesn't tell anyone else.
?

Can I edit a receipt directly?

No. Receipts always mirror their parent invoice. To change a receipt, edit the parent invoice — the receipt updates by itself.

?

Can I change the invoice number after issuing?

Yes. On the edit form, the field Invoice number shows the current number — type a new one and Save. The number on the PDF will change.

Don't do this lightly — accounting uses these numbers as references.

6 · Cancel · Archive · Delete

Three different ways to "make it go away"
?

What's the difference between Cancel, Archive and Delete?

  • Cancel — the invoice was a real mistake (wrong client, wrong deal). Stamps a red "CANCELLED" on the PDF. The number stays in the books — accounting needs the trail.
  • Archive — the invoice is fine but old; you don't want it cluttering the main list. Goes to Archive in the sidebar. Restorable any time.
  • Delete — only for drafts. Drafts have no number, so deleting them leaves no trace. Issued invoices can never be deleted, only archived.
?

How do I cancel an invoice?

Open the issued invoice → click Cancel → type a short reason (visible only inside our team). The PDF re-renders with a red "CANCELLED" stamp at the top.

Paid invoices can't be cancelled. If a paid invoice was wrong, refund the client externally and create a new "credit" invoice for the difference.
?

How do I archive invoices?

  1. On the Invoices list, tick the checkbox of each row to archive.
  2. Click Archive in the bar at the bottom.
  3. They disappear from the main list and show up in Archive in the sidebar.

Need them back? Open Archive, tick them, click Restore.

?

Can I bulk-delete drafts?

Yes. On the Invoices list, tick the drafts → Delete drafts at the bottom → confirm. They're gone forever.

Only drafts. The button is greyed out if you ticked anything that isn't a draft.
?

What about "Delete permanently" in Archive?

That's the nuclear option. It really removes the row from the database. Use only when you're 100% sure — there's no undo. The app refuses to delete an invoice that still has linked receipts; archive (or delete) the receipt first.

7 · Clients (counterparties)

The address book of people who pay us
?

Why "counterparty" instead of "client"?

Old accounting word — same thing. It just means "the other side of the deal". You'll see the word everywhere in the app.

?

How do I add a new client?

Two ways:

  • Quick add — inside the invoice form, click + Quick add. Type their name (Tax ID and address are optional). Save. They're picked for this invoice and saved for next time.
  • Full form — sidebar → Counterparties → + New counterparty. Fill in everything (email, language, notes).
?

What's "Preferred language" on a counterparty?

Decides if their invoice PDF comes out in English or Thai. Pick whichever the client reads. Doesn't change anything inside the app — only the printable PDF.

?

I made a duplicate client by accident — how do I clean up?

You can Hide the duplicate (Counterparties → click on the row → Hide). It vanishes from the picker but old invoices still work.

If both duplicates have invoices already, ask a Super admin — merging clients with invoice history is a manual job.

?

Can I delete a counterparty?

Only if they have no invoices. Otherwise, use Hide.

8 · PDFs and sharing

How files come out and how to send them
?

How do I get the PDF?

Open any issued/paid invoice → there's a PDF box near the top with two buttons:

  • Copy public link — gives you a permanent shareable URL. Best for emails and CRM.
  • Download PDF — saves the file to your computer with a clean filename like Sky Park A-205.pdf.
?

Is the public link safe to share with clients?

Yes. The URL is unguessable (random characters), so nobody can find it without the link. But anyone with the link can open the PDF — don't post it publicly.

?

Can I print directly?

Yes. The PDF preview on the invoice page has a small Print button. Or download and print the file.

?

The PDF looks ugly / something's missing. What now?

Click Regenerate PDF on the invoice page. Usually fixes the issue. If not, screenshot the problem and ping a Super admin.

9 · Searching & finding things

Find any invoice in seconds
?

How do I find a specific invoice?

On the Invoices list, type into the search box at the top. The app searches across:

  • Invoice number (e.g. 23/04 finds all invoices from April 23)
  • Counterparty name
  • Our company name
  • Project name and unit code on line items

Search is case-insensitive and matches partial words.

?

How do I see only drafts (or only paid invoices)?

Use the status chips above the table — All · Draft · Issued · Paid · Cancelled. They combine with search.

?

Can I bookmark a filtered view?

Yes — the filters are in the URL. /admin/invoices?status=draft&q=John shows John's drafts. Bookmark it.

?

I'm looking at the Counterparties list and only see 50 — what about the rest?

It's paginated 50 per page. Use the Prev / Next buttons at the bottom. Or use search to jump to who you need.

10 · Google Drive backup

Your safety net
?

Where else are my invoices stored besides the app?

Every PDF is automatically copied to a Google Drive folder called Invoices Backup. Folder structure is Year / Month / file.pdf. So if anything happens to the app, the files are still there.

?

How are the files named on Drive?

Like this: 23-04-2026-0001_John Smith Sky Park A-205.pdf. Number first (slashes replaced with dashes), then client, project and unit. So you can find any file by searching for the client name, project name, or unit code in Drive.

?

Where do I see the link to the Drive copy?

On any backed-up invoice, the PDF box shows a green dot and Open in Drive →. Click it to open the file in Google Drive.

?

Do I need to do anything to back things up?

No. The app does it for you the moment you Issue, Pay, Edit or Cancel.

11 · Things going wrong

When the app misbehaves
?

I clicked something and the page went blank.

Refresh (Cmd+R or Ctrl+R). 99% of the time it just loads again. If it keeps happening on the same page, screenshot the URL and tell a Super admin.

?

I see a red error message at the top.

Read it — usually it tells you what's wrong (e.g. "Bank account does not belong to the selected company"). Fix the form and try again. If the message is technical and confusing, screenshot it and ping a Super admin.

?

The app is asking me to log in over and over.

Your session probably expired, or your browser's blocking cookies. Try a hard refresh (Cmd+Shift+R / Ctrl+Shift+R), or open the app in a new private window. If that fails, it's a Super admin issue.

?

I deleted a draft by accident.

Drafts can't be undeleted — they're gone for good. Re-create from scratch (or use Duplicate on a similar invoice if there is one).

?

I issued the wrong invoice — the number is gone now, right?

The number is yours forever. Cancel the invoice (it stays in the books with a "CANCELLED" stamp) and make a new one. Numbering keeps moving forward — the next invoice gets the next number.

?

How do I get help?

Three places:

  1. Click Instructions in the sidebar — there's a help article for almost every screen.
  2. Re-read this FAQ.
  3. Ask a Super admin or the team.

12 · Glossary

Every weird word, in one place
Counterparty
The client. The other side of the deal. We use this word everywhere in the app.
Draft
An invoice you started but didn't publish yet. No number, fully editable, can be deleted.
Issued
An invoice that's been published. It has a real number and a polished PDF. Send it to the client.
Paid
An issued invoice that's been marked as received. Comes with a free receipt.
Cancelled
An issued invoice that you reversed. PDF gets a red "CANCELLED" stamp; number is kept.
Archived
An invoice you tucked away to keep the main list clean. Restorable any time.
Receipt
The "thank you, we got the money" PDF. Same number as the invoice plus -R. Made automatically when you mark an invoice paid.
Template
A shortcut that fills in our company, bank and currency for you. Pick the right one for each deal.
Issue date
The date on the invoice. Usually today.
Due date
By when the client should pay. Defaults to issue date + 5 days.
VAT
Value-Added Tax. 7% in Thailand. Turn on for resident companies that need to charge VAT.
WHT
Withholding tax. 3% in Thailand. Turn on if the client withholds it from their payment.
Bank account
One of our company's bank accounts (for the client to pay into). The template auto-picks the right one.
Super admin
An account with extra powers — manage companies, users, help articles. Most people are Users.
User
A regular login. Can do all day-to-day work but can't manage companies or other users.
Public link
A permanent unguessable URL to the PDF. Send to clients, paste in CRM. No login needed to open.
PDF backup
Every PDF is also saved to a Google Drive folder for safety. You don't need to do anything.
Bulk action
Doing the same thing to many invoices at once — tick checkboxes, pick the action at the bottom.

Still stuck?

Click Instructions in the app's sidebar — there's a help article for almost every screen. Then come back to this FAQ. Then ask the team.
