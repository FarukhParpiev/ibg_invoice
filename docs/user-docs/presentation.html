01 What is this app?
In one sentence

It's how we make pretty PDF invoices — and remember who paid us.

Before this app, people made invoices by hand in Word, saved them in folders, and tried to remember the numbers. Things got lost.

Now the app does the boring parts (numbering, math, PDF design) and you just fill in the names and amounts.

Think of it as

A smart notebook
that prints itself.

You write what you sold. It writes the invoice and stamps it with the right number.

04 The life of an invoice

Every invoice goes through these stages. Most stop at "Paid" — that's the happy ending.

Step 1

Draft

You wrote it but didn't send it yet. Free to edit. No number yet.

Step 2

Issued

Locked in. Has a real number. PDF ready to send to the client.

Step 3

Paid 🎉

Money arrived. The app makes a receipt automatically.

Optional

Cancelled

Something went wrong. The PDF gets a red "CANCELLED" stamp.

Why "Draft"? So you can fix typos before the invoice gets a real number. Once it's "Issued", the number is set in stone — that's how accounting stays tidy.

05 How to make a new invoice
  1. Click Invoices on the left, then + New invoice top-right.
  2. Pick a template — that fills in our company and bank for you.
  3. Pick the client. Don't see them? Click + Quick add.
  4. Set the issue date. Due date fills itself in (5 days later).
  5. Add what you sold — usually one line is enough.
  6. Click Save draft. Done.
Remember

A draft is just a sketch.

Nothing is sent, nothing is final. You can come back tomorrow and finish it. The number only gets stamped on when you click "Issue".

07 Adding a client (we call them "counterparty")

Quick way

Inside the invoice form, click + Quick add next to the client picker.

Type their name (Tax ID and address are optional — fill in later).

Save → they're picked for this invoice and saved for next time.

Full way

Use this when you have time and want to add full details (email, language preference, notes).

Click Counterparties on the left → + New counterparty → fill in the form.

Tip: Preferred language decides if their PDF comes out in English or Thai.

Already in the list? Always pick the existing one. Never create a duplicate — accounting has to merge them later by hand.

08 Issuing — making it real

When the invoice is ready to send, open it and click the big black Issue button.

Five things happen at once:

  1. The next number is stamped on (e.g. 23/04/2026-0001).
  2. Today's exchange rate is locked in (if needed).
  3. A polished PDF appears.
  4. The PDF is also saved to Google Drive backup.
  5. Status changes from "Draft" to "Issued".
3 sec

Average time from clicking Issue to having a sendable PDF.

Don't confuse "Generate PDF" with "Issue".
Generate PDF on a draft only makes a preview. Only Issue publishes the invoice with a real number.

09 Got paid? Click one button.

Open the issued invoice → click the green Mark as paid (+ receipt) button.

It will ask for the payment date (default: today). After that:

  1. The invoice flips to Paid.
  2. A new receipt is born — same number plus "-R".
  3. Both PDFs are made and backed up.
  4. The receipt shows up under Receipts on the left.
Pinky promise

You never write a receipt by hand.

The app does it. Always. If you need to share the receipt link with the client, click Copy public link on the receipt page.

10 Made a mistake? Pick the right fix.

Wrong amount, typo, wrong line

Edit the invoice — yes, even after it's issued or paid. The PDF and receipt re-generate automatically.

A yellow banner reminds you that the invoice is published.

Whole invoice was a mistake

Cancel it. The PDF gets a red "CANCELLED" stamp. The number is kept (accounting needs the trail).

Already paid? You can't cancel — refund externally and make a new credit invoice.

Don't want it in the main list anymore

Archive it. Goes to Archive in the sidebar. You can restore any time.

It was just an unwanted draft

Delete the draft. Drafts have no number, so deleting them leaves no trail. Issued invoices can never be deleted, only archived.
